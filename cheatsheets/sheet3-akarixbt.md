**Note:** Not REA-specific. Useful as the **manager contract** when staffing an REA investigation.

## Copyable structure (abbreviated)

- `<goal>` — Finish `{{PROJECT}}` with smallest useful team; proof required; stop when complete/blocked/needs approval.
- `<project_brief>` — PROJECT, OUTCOME, DEADLINE, AUDIENCE, SUCCESS_CRITERIA, TOOLS, SOURCES, CONSTRAINTS.
- `<manager_contract>` — One manager; task board; shared record; one blocking question max.
- `<team_design>` — Roles only as needed: manager, researcher, engineer, designer, writer, QA, ops.
- `<agent_contract>` — Per bot: name, mission, inputs, tools, artifact, acceptance, proof, stop, handoff.
- `<execution_flow>` — Packages → parallel/order → artifact+evidence+status → repair → board → escalate blockers only.
- `<verification>` — No proof, no done. Separate reviewer for high-impact. Fluent prose ≠ evidence.
- `<boundaries>` — No publish/send/buy/deploy/delete/grant without explicit approval.
- `<completion_gate>` — Owners, openable deliverables, evidence, tests, named gaps, pending approvals.
- `<handoff>` — Status, team, artifacts, evidence, decisions/gaps, approvals, next safe action.

## Mapping to REA work

Treat REA tools as `{{TOOLS}}`. Evidence IDs (`ev_…`) and unknowns (`unk_…`) are the proof artifacts. Do not claim a feature understood until Evidence cites file/function/call and residual unknowns are listed.
