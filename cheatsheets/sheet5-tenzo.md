# S6 — Order-book trading discipline (@0xTenzo)

**Source:** https://x.com/0xTenzo/status/2107538907147440582  
**Media:** amplify video (preview `sheet5-tenzo-preview.jpg`); full text from X `note_tweet`.  
**Relevance to REA:** Indirect. Ops discipline that maps to "no evidence → no claim / no trade."

## Rules claimed in the post

1. Measure real depth on both sides before sizing.  
2. Never enter bigger than you can exit without slippage.  
3. Sit on the bid instead of hitting market.  
4. Won't drag a position into a big sell wall.  
5. Keep margin at a fraction of deposit (post: 37–59%); rest free.  
6. Prefer doing nothing when the book is thin.  
7. Leverage is not the edge; the book is.  
8. Anecdote numbers ($37 → $8,423) are **not verified** and must not be treated as expected returns.

## Transfer into REA / desk practice

- Thin evidence ≡ thin book: refuse the trade / refuse the claim.  
- Size to exit path: only claim what Evidence can support (`list_unknowns` for gaps).  
- Free buffer: keep risk budget (Kraken spot rules) independent of strategy enthusiasm.
